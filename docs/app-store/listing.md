**Description** (4000 max)

```
Azkar helps you keep your prayers, your azkar and your Quran reading every day, and shows you how you are doing over time.

PRAYER TIMES
• Accurate prayer times for where you are, with all the common calculation methods and both Asr methods
• A live countdown to the next prayer
• Log each prayer as on time, in congregation or made up, with one tap
• Reminders, and an adhan alarm that sounds even in silent mode
• Qibla compass

AZKAR
• Morning, evening and before-sleep azkar, and the azkar after each prayer
• One dhikr at a time, counted with a tap, with your progress kept every day
• A tasbih counter, with your own dhikr if you wish

THE QURAN
• The mushaf in its 604 Medina pages
• Mark where you stopped, and follow your khatmah
• Set a date to finish a khatmah and see the pages a day it takes
• Keep track of the surahs you have memorised and when you last reviewed them

TRACKER
• Streaks for the five prayers and the azkar
• Prayers and fasts to make up, including those from before you started
• Fasting, Ramadan, tarawih and the last ten nights
• Charity and zakat, with a reminder on your yearly zakat date
• Your own duas, brought forward on the day of Arafah, in the last ten nights and on Friday afternoons
• A review of each Hijri year
• Export to a spreadsheet, and automatic backups to a folder you choose

ON YOUR IPHONE AND APPLE WATCH
• Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets to tick off today's prayers
• A Live Activity for the next prayer
• An Apple Watch app to log prayers and count tasbih
• Siri and Shortcuts

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads and no tracking. Everything you record stays on your iPhone.

Available in English and Arabic.
```

```
No sign-in is needed. Location is optional and used only on the device, for prayer times and the Qibla; a city can also be chosen by name. The adhan alarm uses iOS alarms (AlarmKit) and asks for permission when first turned on. All records are stored on the device. The only network service is optional: if the user adds the "Today's prayers" Apple Wallet card (Settings > Apple Wallet), the app sends that card's content to our signing server (azkar-wallet.ahmedwalid4499.workers.dev), which signs the pass and keeps nothing.
```
